Who we are
Energy+ is a vendor neutral energy company & not tied to any
manufacturer, contractor, or utility. We work with engineers trusted by
Sandia National Laboratories, the Department of Defense, and the
Department of Energy to verify best-in-class retrofits that eliminate
energy overspend. We represent and advocate for the end client.

Our most popular solutions for buildings like yours:
  - Compressor retrofits that eliminate 20 to 50% of HVAC/R overspend
  - Energy harmonization. Your utility penalizes you for poor power
    quality, and left unaddressed it damages your equipment.
  - Peak shaving to cut your peak demand charges

How we work, in three steps

1. Diagnose. We start with your utility bills and your HVAC and
   Refrigeration equipment, not a product catalog. That tells us exactly
   where the money is going and where it is being wasted.

2. Recommend only what meets your ROI. You tell us the payback or return
   threshold your business needs. We only put forward solutions that
   clear it. If nothing clears it, we tell you that too.

3. Fund it so it cash flows from day one. We line up the financing,
   utility rebates, and tax incentives that fit the project, so the
   savings cover the cost from the first month rather than years later.
